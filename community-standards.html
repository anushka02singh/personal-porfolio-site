Overview

The Office of Community Standards already had a page inside another site. It needed to become its own individual site. The direction was minimal and the ask was simple and quick, so the challenge was keeping it that way without letting it look generic.

What I Did

  • Took the existing page and built it out into a full standalone site
  • Set up the navigation around Home and About Us (with Staff Leads), plus the office's areas: Student Conduct, Compliance & Title IX, Restorative Practices, the Bias Outreach, Restoration and Education Team, and Academic Integrity
  • Made the anonymous reporting option easy to spot, with a text number and a QR code
  • Kept the layout accessible, with clear headings, readable contrast and plain navigation, while giving it its own look

Status

Live at communitystandards.rutgers.edu.
