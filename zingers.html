Overview

TikTok grew not just because of its algorithm but because of its comment sections, which are often funnier than the videos themselves and even start trends, like the sparkle-emoji text style. Zingers is a new app idea that turns the comment section into an app of its own.

The Idea

  • Prompt and reply: people post a prompt, and the replies are the content
  • Ranked replies: the funniest replies rise to the top
  • Profiles: a commenter's best replies build a record of their wit
  • Identity: a question to test, named accounts versus anonymous handles

It sits against TikTok comments, Reddit and anonymous campus apps like Yik Yak, which don't make the reply itself the main unit.

The Plan

  • Pitch the idea and ground it in research on humor, belonging and anonymous platforms
  • Build a clickable Figma prototype of the core loop: post a prompt, reply, find the top replies, open a profile
  • Make two versions that differ only in identity (named versus anonymous) to compare
  • Run short think-aloud sessions with a few classmates and note what changes between versions
