Overview

The Business Office of Student Affairs helps departments buy things, record transactions, plan budgets and report on them. Its site is being built in Drupal on Pantheon, and the goal is simple: explain what the office does in plain language, and get each department to the right service quickly.

What I'm Doing

  • Building the full site in Drupal on a Pantheon development environment
  • Writing the page structure around a short Mission followed by Core services — four ways the office supports a department
  • Turning each service — Purchasing & Concur assistance, Transactional Accounting, Budgeting & Forecasting, Financial Reporting — into its own card with an icon, a one-line plain-language summary, and a short list of what's covered
  • Keeping the page easy to scan, using Rutgers red for the card rules and headings so it feels at home in the Student Affairs family of sites

Status

In progress. The site lives on a Pantheon dev environment and isn't public yet.
