Overview

A healthcare client needed a site that read as trustworthy before it read as anything else — one of several independent builds I've taken on since 2017, from first conversation to final handoff.

What I Did

I consulted directly with the client to understand their practice, their audience, and their brand identity, then translated that into a finished website — handling layout, visual design, and build from start to delivery.

  • Met with the client to scope the project and understand their goals
  • Designed a layout and visual identity aligned with a healthcare practice's needs (clarity, trust, easy navigation)
  • Built and delivered the finished site directly to the client
