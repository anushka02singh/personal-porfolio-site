Overview

The department handed me a simple Word document: a list of what the site should hold, which referred back to an older site that was really just a page of buttons. I took that and built a full site from the ground up — modern, still simple and accessible, but unmistakably its own thing rather than a template.

What I Did

  • Built the whole site from the Word document — page structure, layout and content organization
  • Created a row of four large quick-action buttons for the tasks people come for: myRutgers Portal, Position Request, Division Absence and Contact SA HR Director
  • Laid out the University Department section in three columns: HR & Employment and Compliance & Finance as lists of link cards with a hover arrow, with a building illustration anchoring the middle
  • Wrote everything in plain HTML and CSS using flexbox, because the CMS editor strips scripts, and kept the markup semantic: real headings, real lists, real links, large click targets

Status

Finished and waiting on the department's approval. It's on a Pantheon dev site, so it isn't public yet.
