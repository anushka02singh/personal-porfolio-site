Rutgers Welcome Week Website

Role Web Design Intern Manager
Type Full Site Build
Timeline 2026
Org Student Affairs, Rutgers University

Overview

Homepage and section builds for Rutgers–New Brunswick's Welcome Week site — a resource hub meant to give incoming and returning students one clear starting point to orient themselves before move-in, rather than needing to hunt across a dozen different department pages.

What I Built

I worked on the layout and content structure for the homepage and several key sections:

  • Homepage hero and introductory "Rutgers Welcome" section, with embedded video
  • A Facility Hours quick-access hub covering dining, student centers, health centers, and recreation
  • A Navigating Campus section with campus map access and bus route resources
  • An Arrival & Move-In section with move-in guidance
  • An events listing pulling in upcoming Welcome Week programming
  • A "Get Involved" teaser directing students toward the fuller events schedule

Status

This site is now live: welcome.rutgers.edu.
