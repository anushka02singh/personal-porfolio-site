Fellowship Project: Redesigning Forest

Role UX Researcher & Designer
Type UI/UX Case Study
Timeline 2021–2022
Tools Figma, User Interviews, Usability Testing

Overview

Completed as part of my UI/UX Fellowship, this project was a full redesign of Forest — a productivity app that helps users stay off their phones by "growing" a virtual tree during a focus session. I worked with a small team to identify usability problems in the existing app and redesign the experience end-to-end.

What I Did

I conducted user research on the existing app — including user interviews, surveys, and usability testing — to identify friction points and opportunities for improvement. From there, I translated those findings into paper wireframes and a high-fidelity Figma prototype.

  • Ran usability testing sessions on the existing app to identify pain points
  • Synthesized survey and interview findings into concrete design opportunities
  • Built paper wireframes to explore layout and flow quickly before moving to Figma
  • Designed a high-fidelity Figma prototype reflecting the redesign

New Features We Proposed

As a team, we identified two features to extend the app's core focus mechanic:

  • Friend notifications — alerting users when friends are also using the app, adding a layer of social accountability
  • Embedded music apps — letting users play music from within Forest itself, reducing the temptation to leave the app (and their focus session) to open a separate music app

Outcome

This project was my first end-to-end UX process — research through prototype — and it's where I built the research and usability-testing habits I still bring to my work today.
