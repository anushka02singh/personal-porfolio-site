Overview

The New Jersey Higher Education Mental Health Summit 2027 is a one-day conference at Rutgers–New Brunswick for leaders and front-line practitioners from colleges across the state, on the theme “Institutional Vitality.” The site had to carry everything an attendee needs, and the material I was given to build it from was a confusing document.

What I Did

  • Worked back and forth with my supervisor to turn a confusing source document into a clear plan for the site
  • Created every page: Home, Summit Schedule, Campus Location Information, FAQs, Call for Programs and Keynote Speaker, with registration linked out
  • Took full direction of the seminar page — how it looks, how it reads, and where each piece of information lives
  • Kept the pages simple and easy to navigate for attendees visiting for one specific day

Status

Live at njhemhs2027.rutgers.edu ahead of the summit on January 7, 2027 (rain date January 8).
