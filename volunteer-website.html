Overview

For six months I was the only person keeping YesMiss, Inc.'s website honest — their programs, volunteer openings, and events, all current, all accurate, with no one else to catch what I missed.

What I Did

  • Maintained weekly to-do lists to plan and prioritize ongoing site updates
  • Regularly updated event, program, and volunteer information to keep the site current
  • Ran routine functionality checks — testing links, forms, and page load — to catch issues early
  • Collaborated with the marketing team and graphic designers to keep branding consistent across platforms
  • Participated in biweekly team meetings to stay aligned on progress and priorities

Status

My internship with YesMiss concluded in March 2025. I no longer have active access to the live site.
