Overview

YesMiss, Inc. is a non-profit, and for six months I was part of their team. The site was a living thing: events, programs and volunteer information changed all the time, and my job was to keep it up to date by working with everyone who had something to add.

What I Did

  • Updated the site constantly, keeping events, programs and volunteer information current
  • Worked with the whole team — marketing, graphic designers and program staff — so what went online matched what was happening
  • Kept weekly to-do lists to plan and prioritize site updates
  • Ran routine functionality checks on links, forms and page load to catch issues early
  • Joined biweekly team meetings to stay aligned on priorities

Status

My internship with YesMiss concluded in March 2025. I no longer have active access to the live site.
