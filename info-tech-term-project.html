Overview

The final term project for my graduate Information Technologies course at Rutgers. It brings the whole semester together into one functional, responsive site with four sections: Home, Info Tech, Interests and About. I built it front to back: layout and design, the interactive JavaScript, and the PHP and MySQL survey system.

Features

  • A responsive Bootstrap 4 layout with a custom-themed, collapsible mobile navigation bar
  • An open-source technology evaluation of Drupal, with rollover images and comparison cards, informed by my day-to-day work managing Drupal sites at Rutgers Student Affairs
  • A survey system: a client-validated preference form, plus a usability-rating survey that submits to a MySQL database through a prepared-statement PHP backend, with results aggregated and shown dynamically
  • JavaScript interactions: image rollovers, a Bootstrap carousel with custom captions, collapsible panels, tabbed content and active-nav highlighting
  • A personal Interests section: a rom-com-themed showcase with a hero carousel, embedded trailers and hover-reveal movie cards

Status

Published on GitHub. The database credentials are deliberately left out of the repo, with an example config file to copy for running the PHP and MySQL parts locally.
